What was missing
✗ No topic classification
Conversation entered as undifferentiated noise. No BU reporting, no volume trends, no way to know hardship cases were spiking.
✗ Complaint relied on one agent's judgment
If that Collections officer hadn't acted, CMOSxxxx would never have been raised. No systematic scoring of every interaction.
✗ Unresolved closure not automatically detected
No system flagged that CMOSxxxx was closed without resolution. The IRE event sat with empty fields. Only chance surfaced it.
✗ IRE fields populated manually — weeks late
Reportability, root cause, L1/L2, vulnerability, financial loss — all required specialist analyst time. Inconsistent. Too slow for ASIC windows.
✗ IDR obligation breach never tested
No written response issued after hardship decision. No automated IDR test ran. Breach invisible until customer escalated — 14 days overdue.
✗ 2LOD had no independent view
Second Line relied entirely on 1LOD reporting. No governed analytics. No systematic oversight of this or thousands of similar interactions.
